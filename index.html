Ongoing · Aug 2026 – Vision Lab · IIT Dharwad

Ambiguity-Aware Language-Guided Object Grounding for Robotic Pick-and-Place

An object-centric referring segmentation framework that finds the target object from a natural-language instruction — and decides whether to act, reject, or ask for clarification when several objects match.

16Mparam Transformer grounder
303Kphrase–image pairs (D4)
80.6%ambiguous-query success, up from 3.0%
0.989AUROC ambiguity detection
83.6%single-target accuracy on LVIS
  • 16M-parameter Transformer-based grounder over frozen SAM and CLIP features, geometric attributes and pairwise object relationships.
  • Constructed D4 — 303K phrase–image pairs across 19,994 COCO images — and extended training with LVIS queries for better generalization across categories.
  • Ambiguity resolution via duplicate-mask grouping, cardinality estimation and scene-grounded clarification questions — at roughly one question per request.
  • Ongoing: an end-to-end interactive assistant with iterative clarification and language-guided placement-point estimation.

Pipeline

  1. 01 Instruction + scene
  2. 02 SAM masks · CLIP features
  3. 03 Geometry + relations
  4. 04 Transformer grounder
  5. 05 Act · Reject · Clarify

Semi-Automated Estimation of Above-Ground Biomass in Agroforestry

Presented at IEEE IGARSS 2026, Washington D.C.; awaiting publication in the conference proceedings.

  • Pipeline using the Segment Anything Model for tree crown and shadow segmentation from very high-resolution satellite imagery.
  • Post-processing that uses shadow geometry to estimate tree height and biomass relative to a reference tree.
  • Scalable biomass assessment with far less dependence on manual field surveys.

Follow-up work Jan 2026 – May 2026

Unsupervised Segmentation of High-Resolution Agroforestry Plots

Extended the IGARSS work to remove its dependence on labeled data with a fully unsupervised pipeline.

  • DINOv2 embeddings for coarse segmentation maps with no labeled training data, refined with Dense CRF.
  • Instance separation via distance transform and a flooding-based algorithm for individual tree detection.
  • Handles high intra-class variability and low inter-class separability in dense canopy.
